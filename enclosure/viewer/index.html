Desk Display Dock

A low wedge for the Waveshare 12.3″ 1920×720 touchscreen. The screen lies back at 30° from the desk, flush with the shell, above a plain 30 mm front face. Inside are a Raspberry Pi 4 and an ESP32 test bench. GPIO comes out on the right side and the ESP32 on the left. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom.
